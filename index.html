Top Places to Visit in Uganda

  • Bwindi Impenetrable National Park – Famous for gorilla trekking and bird watching.
  • Murchison Falls National Park – Home to the mighty Nile waterfall and diverse wildlife.
  • Queen Elizabeth National Park – Known for tree-climbing lions and boat safaris.
  • Lake Victoria – Africa’s largest lake, great for fishing and island tours.
  • Jinja – Source of the Nile – Adventure capital with rafting and bungee jumping.
  • Rwenzori Mountains National Park – Snow-capped peaks and challenging hikes.
  • Lake Bunyonyi – Scenic lake with 29 islands, perfect for relaxation.
  • Sipi Falls – Stunning waterfalls on Mount Elgon slopes.
  • Kampala City – Vibrant capital with culture, markets, and nightlife.
  • Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary – The only place to see wild rhinos in Uganda.
